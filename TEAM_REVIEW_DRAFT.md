# BIG Partner Portal Team Review

This branch is an internal review draft for Brokerage Interconnect Group.

## Goal
Create a white-label telecom partner portal for agents, MSPs, IT consultants, carriers, and enterprise clients.

## Product Focus
- Partner onboarding
- Telecom quote intake
- Quote pipeline tracking
- MRR visibility
- Commission forecasting
- Carrier marketplace
- White-label settings

## Brand Direction
BIG should feel like an enterprise SaaS platform, not a construction or hardware company.

Tagline: The white-label distribution engine for telecom revenue.

## Team Review Questions
- Is the positioning correct?
- Which user should we serve first: agent, MSP, carrier, or enterprise buyer?
- What fields are required for a quote request?
- What commission details should be shown first?
